About

A native Spotify client for iOS 5 and 6 on iPhone, iPod touch and iPad, based on riff by Second Track. It runs entirely on the device. Spotify Premium is required.

Features

  • Your playlists, albums, artists and liked songs
  • Queue with drag to reorder
  • Search, artist and album pages
  • Offline downloads and a song cache
  • Lock screen controls and background playback
  • New in 1.2.2A Classic iOS skin (Settings › Skin), songs that play without opening the full player, a faster Home, and a fix for networks that break secure connections
  • New in 1.2.0iOS 5 support, a native iPad layout (portrait and landscape), the full 4-inch screen on iPhone 5 and iPod touch 5, and songs that start playing while they download

Installing

Spotify6 installs as a normal App Store style app (it needs AppSync Unified). Updating keeps your login and downloads. Removing the package also removes the app and its data.

Unofficial and not affiliated with Spotify. It uses undocumented Spotify interfaces with your own account, which may be against Spotify's Terms of Use. Use at your own risk.
