About this beta

A test version of the next big update of Spotify6 (2.0, which will be called Spotty). It installs as its own app, Spotty (Beta), next to Spotify6, so your normal app keeps working. You log in once in the beta. Spotify Premium is required.

What's new in beta 5

  • Crash reportsIf the app crashed, the next start asks whether to send a crash report to the developer
  • Faster on older devicesConnections are reused, songs start sooner, and iOS 3 no longer freezes while covers load
  • Audio qualitySeparate Streaming and Downloads settings (Settings › Audio quality). Streaming starts at Low on iPhone 2G/3G and iPod touch 1G/2G
  • iPad on iOS 3.2No more hangs at start
  • EmojiEmoji your iOS version can't show are left out of titles instead of showing as boxes

In this beta

  • More iOS versionsRuns on iOS 3.1 to 10 (iPhone, iPod touch and iPad)
  • Older devicesiPhone 2G/3G and iPod touch 1G/2G are supported by the app
  • Send logSettings › Debug info › Send log uploads the app log and gives you a short code for the developer

Please test

  • Home, Library, Search and opening playlists and albums
  • Playing songs, skipping, the queue and downloads
  • Swipe right on a song (add to queue) and long-press a song (menu)
  • On iOS 8, 9 and 10: everything. Nobody has tried these yet.

Something wrong?

If the app crashed, just open it again and tap Send when it asks. For other problems, right after it happens (before closing the app): open Settings › Debug info, tap Send log and send the code it shows to the developer. The log is uploaded to paste.rs, a public text site; your Spotify account name, user ID and login data are removed first.

Known issues

  • The first start on older devices can take a while

Installing

Needs AppSync for your iOS version. If Cydia can't find it, add the repo below (Sources › Edit › Add):

  • iOS 5 to 10: AppSync Unifiedhttps://lukezgd.github.io/repo/
  • iOS 4: AppSync for iOS 4.0http://repo.legacyios.com/
  • iOS 3: AppSync for OS 3.1 or OS 3.2https://cydia.invoxiplaygames.uk/ios3/ (or http://ios3.party/)

These repos are run by other people. I'm not affiliated with them; they are listed only to help you find AppSync, and I take no responsibility for their content. Add and install at your own risk.

Updating the beta keeps its login and downloads. Removing the package also removes the beta app and its data. Spotify6 is not touched.
